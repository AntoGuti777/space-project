About the Experience

Learning Through Exploration

Explore the Solar System is designed for curious students and casual space enthusiasts who want a welcoming introduction to the eight planets. Its goal is to make planetary comparison visual, interactive, and easy to understand.

Scientific details are presented alongside clearly labeled cultural astrology notes. Astrology reflects historical traditions and is not presented as science. Planetary facts are based primarily on NASA Solar System Exploration resources.

  • ✦ Eight interactive planet profiles
  • ✦ Exploration progress tracking
  • ✦ Rotating educational space facts
  • ✦ Responsive and keyboard-friendly design
